PhD Researcher · Carbon Capture & Membrane Technology

I'm a PhD Researcher at the University of Bath within the EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training in Green Industrial Futures. My research focuses on one-pot chemical vapour deposition synthesis of high-performance membranes for CO₂ capture, contributing to next-generation carbon capture technologies.

I recently graduated from Swansea University with a First-Class MEng in Chemical Engineering, establishing a strong foundation in engineering design, process modelling and advanced characterisation techniques.

I'm keen to connect with researchers and industry professionals working in carbon capture, membrane technology and sustainable process engineering.
